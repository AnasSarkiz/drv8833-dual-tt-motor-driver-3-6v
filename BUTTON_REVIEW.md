# Motor-disable button — alpha.6

SW1 is a normally-open momentary tactile button, labeled **HOLD TO DISABLE**. Holding it pulls the DRV8833 ENABLE/nSLEEP input low and disables both H-bridges. Motors coast; the board remains powered. Releasing it returns control to the external host and can restart the motors after the driver's wake delay. This is a local operating control, not a latching stop, active brake, power disconnect or emergency-stop circuit. No new firmware is required for its basic function.

Comparable DRV8833 carriers expose sleep/enable control, as in the [Adafruit pinout guide](https://learn.adafruit.com/adafruit-drv8833-dc-stepper-motor-driver-breakout-board/pinouts) and [Pololu carrier](https://www.pololu.com/product/2130). A button is an optional convenience; these references do not establish a universal button convention. The chosen momentary disable function uses the [TI nSLEEP behavior](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/drv8833.pdf), sections 7.3.4 and 7.4.

## Electrical and mechanical review

- **SW1: XKB TS-1187A-B-A-B, JLC C318884**, Basic, top SMT, 5.1 × 5.1 × 1.5 mm body; 50 mA / 12 V DC contact rating. The [manufacturer drawing](references/TS-1187A-B-A-B.pdf) was visually reviewed. A–B (pins 1–2) and C–D (pins 3–4) are permanent pairs; pressing connects the two pairs. A/B connect to ENABLE, C/D to GND. The native pushbutton model explicitly includes these pairs.
- The supplier footprint has 1.00 × 0.75 mm pads at X=±3.00 mm, Y=±1.849882 mm. The drawing's suggested land pattern gives X=±3.00 mm, Y=±1.875 mm; the 0.025118 mm Y difference is inside its general ±0.1 mm tolerance. The imported body courtyard is retained. Parts-engine pin-1 matching verifies the supplied CPL orientation; the final assembly preview must confirm the contact-row axis, since a 90° error would bridge ENABLE to ground.
- **R8 changes from 330 Ω to 2.2 kΩ (C4190)**. With a 5.25 V host and −1% resistance, pressing draws at most 2.411 mA from HOST_ENABLE and dissipates about 12.66 mW in R8 (100 mW part). Require a host GPIO that tolerates this source current. At the switch's specified 100 mΩ maximum contact resistance, ENABLE is below 0.25 mV, well under the 0.5 V input-low limit.
- **R13 changes from 10 kΩ to 100 kΩ (C25803)**. At host VOH=2.8 V, R8=+1%, R13=−1%, and the datasheet's stated 13 µA nSLEEP current, ENABLE is at least 2.710 V, above the 2.5 V threshold. This is a calculation at the specified input-current conditions, not a universal host guarantee. R13 and the driver's internal pull-down retain the disconnected-host sleep default. The four motor-command inputs retain 330 Ω / 10 kΩ networks.
- SW1 center is (17,24) mm on top, away from the corner mounting keepout and host connectors. Its two added ENABLE paths use 0.25 mm copper and two 0.30/0.45 mm vias. All previous saved power and signal paths remain unchanged. Ground contacts join the checked top pour. Four-layer, 75 × 60 mm board size and mounting pattern are unchanged.

## Verification and use

Native netlist and placement DRC: zero errors/warnings. Full schema, build, mechanical and native routing checks pass. The existing advisory C7 rotation suggestion remains. All 26 board tests / 1,139 assertions pass, including released/pressed switch contact topology, GPIO current budget, enable-high margin, ground continuity and strict supplier orientation. All four copper layers and the control schematic were visually inspected. Gerber-mode shorts at 40 px/mm reports no shorts. Final exports contain 40 top SMT placements, seven manual connectors, 110 top stencil apertures and no bottom paste.

Bench checks: with motors initially disconnected, measure ENABLE released/pressed at both supported host voltages; verify the host can source the pressed current. Test with wheels lifted: hold SW1 to remove drive, then confirm expected restart behavior on release. Verify wake timing, contact bounce and supply transients before normal operation. Use the external controller or a separate suitable power disconnect when a maintained stop is required. The existing motor/load/thermal test plan still applies.
